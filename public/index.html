Which links work?

Public YouTube videos and Shorts, TikTok videos, Instagram Reels and posts, and Twitch clips and VODs. Private or login-only content won't download. Sites change often, so the app updates its downloader automatically.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

That notice appears for new apps from small developers. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Some antivirus tools also check a new app on its first launch; if it asks for your key a second time, just enter it again.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. Captioning a 30-second clip takes well under a minute on a typical laptop. The first run downloads a speech model once. Captions are tuned for English; other languages can be chosen in the app.

Is the score accurate?

It measures things known to matter for short-form: how fast the hook lands, pacing, length, captions and format. It can't know the algorithm or your audience, so treat it as editing guidance.
