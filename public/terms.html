What you're buying

ClipHeist is a desktop app for Windows 10 and 11. One payment of £4.99 gives you a licence key for personal use, which activates the app on up to 2 PCs. There is no subscription. Activating a key needs an internet connection; captioning and exporting then run on your PC.

Delivery

Your licence key and download link are shown on screen immediately after payment. Please save the key. If you lose it, email us from the address you paid with and we'll resend it.

Privacy

Payments. Checkout is handled by Stripe. We never see your card details. Stripe shares your email address and payment status with us so we can deliver and support your order.

Activation. When you activate, the app sends your licence key and an anonymous code derived from your PC to our server, so one key can't be used on more than 2 PCs. It contains no personal files or information.

Your clips. Videos, captions and exports stay on your PC. They are not uploaded to us. The app contacts the video site you paste a link from, and downloads a speech model and downloader updates from their public sources.

This website uses no advertising or tracking cookies.
